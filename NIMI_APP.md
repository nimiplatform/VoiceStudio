# VoiceStudio

Create speech and voices, transcribe recordings, dub video and prepare audiobooks in one audio workspace.

## Start working

Open VoiceStudio from Nimi Home and keep Nimi running. Choose the audio task, configure its Nimi resources and provide the required text, recording or video. Voice creation and conversion may require reference audio; use material you have permission to use.

Preview the result before exporting. Check names, pronunciation, transcription text and timing yourself. Model and service support varies by task; an available model is not a promise that every voice control or format is supported.

## Data and conditions

The App retains its projects and output files in its local workspace. AI processing follows your selected Nimi Local or Cloud resources. Cloud processing sends the input needed for that task to the selected service; local storage does not imply that inference is local.

Generated voices are synthetic. Use them with appropriate consent and make their synthetic origin clear when sharing them. Keep original recordings and export important finished work before changing workspaces or removing local data.

For help, contact the App provider with the task, input format and error message; do not include private recordings or credentials.
